The AI Course for Engineers is a one-day workshop that I teach at INFOTIV. The workshop explains how AI and large language models (LLMs) work. With this knowledge, engineers can use these tools with a plan, not by luck.

The workshop moves the participants from "vibe coding" to agentic engineering. The participants write specifications, set rules and checks, run agents on real code and verify the results. The workshop does not give "magic prompts".

#### Target audience

* Developers
* Testers and quality assurance engineers
* Engineers in safety-critical and regulated domains
* Technical leads and engineering managers

#### Course content

* **Intro**: What the day is and what it is not. A demonstration of a game that an agent made from one prompt.
* **Inside the box**: AI, machine learning and deep learning. The participants train a small neural network by hand.
* **Language models**: Tokens, embeddings, transformers, temperature and context windows.
* **Working with the model**: Prompt engineering and how to write a specification that the model can follow.
* **Harness, tools and agents**: Tool calling, Claude Code, the Model Context Protocol (MCP), skills and the command line.
* **Context engineering and memory**: What to put in the context of the model and what to leave out. Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
* **Security and brakes**: Sandboxes, credentials and prompt injection.
* **Limits, failures and verification**: Hallucination, bias, tests and benchmarks.
* **Ethics, law and obligations**: Real incidents, the EU AI Act and standards.
* **Models, cost and running locally**: Token cost, open and closed models, local models, quantization and fine-tuning.

The participants answer quiz questions on their phones during the day.
